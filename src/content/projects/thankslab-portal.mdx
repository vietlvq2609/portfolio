Thankslab Portal is a customized platform built for welfare and disability-support facilities
in Japan, replacing a third-party ERP/SaaS product the client previously relied on.

## My role

I led the end-to-end design and development of the platform, working directly with Japanese
clients through IT coordinators (Comtor) in a SCRUM process — translating real operational
needs from the welfare-facility domain into a working product.

## Stack & practices

- Backend and frontend built with Laravel and React
- SQL schema design and maintenance
- Docker-based environments, deployed to Linux servers (EC2, VPS) via SSH
- Unit and feature test coverage as part of the delivery workflow

This is client-owned software, so there's no public repo or live demo — the notes above
describe my role and the architecture at a level that stays outside any client-confidential
specifics.
